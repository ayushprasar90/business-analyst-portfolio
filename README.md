# 📊 Featured Project

## 🔷 DivyaVision Dashboard (CEO-Level Business Tracking)

### 🧩 Problem Statement
Tracking performance across multiple projects was challenging due to lack of centralized and real-time visibility.  
This led to:
- Delayed decision-making  
- Inefficient performance tracking  
- Missed revenue optimization opportunities  

---

### 🚀 What I Did
- Designed and developed a **CEO-level business dashboard**
- Consolidated data across multiple projects into a single view  
- Built structured reporting across key time frames:
  - DOD (Day-on-Day)  
  - WOW (Week-on-Week)  
  - MTD (Month-to-Date)  
  - MOM (Month-on-Month)  
  - LTD (Life-to-Date)  

---

### 📈 Key Metrics Covered
- Sales performance (Project & Company level)  
- Operations tracking (Efficiency & performance)  
- Revenue monitoring  
- P&L analysis (Project-level profitability)  
- Retention cohort analysis  
- Business projections & forecasting  

---

### 🛠 Tools & Approach
